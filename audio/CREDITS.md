# KoaCoast audio

The clips in `sounds/` are original procedural sound effects synthesized for this
project during the Hawaiian color and audio pass. The sounds use seeded noise,
filtered transients, and synthesized bird calls. They contain no recordings or
samples from third-party libraries. The generated audio is provided under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- Canopy wind, exposed wind, Pacific surf, insects, freshwater, rapids, falls,
  impact, and pool ambience use overlapping loop seams.
- Forest, distant, and coastal calls are synthesized wildlife-like cues.
- Ground contacts and vegetation brushes have two generated takes each.

Press **F7** in-game to adjust Ambient and Effects volumes. The selections
are retained locally. Music has its own bus for future tracks.
